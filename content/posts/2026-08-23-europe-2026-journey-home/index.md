## Day 1 - Sunday 23 August

Camp site: Aire de Camping Car Vallee de la Scarpe, Arras, France


Early start for the 430 mile journey to Aire de Camping Car Vallee de la Scarpe. Thankfully temperature low 20's and a relatively decent nights sleep. No milk for tea though, poor start tut tut. Left site around 8:45 and drove the route including all tolls. Must have been a total of around £65 all in. Christ. Stopped en route for various coffee stops and baps that were made by Mel. Another fuel fill up - over £2 per litre! Around £130 to fill. Will total all of the costs up at the end, but the diesel has been a real killer and would definitely make us re-evaluate this type of trip again. On the final straight to the aire, the sat nav took us down a one way street that narrowed to a pedestrian bridge about 2 metres wide. We nearly got wedged and were re-directed out by an old French baboushka. Finally arrived around 8 hours later at the aire. £12 for an overnight stop and all services included - pretty damn good. We freshened up and went straight out to the beautiful medieval town of Arras. There was a huge market on with very interesting goods on display and the market square was rammed! We found a cracking pub - The Melting Pot to watch the Newcastle vs Liverpool match and settled in for a good few hours. Mel could not wait for the big game. The ale choice was exceptional! There was around 30 fine Belgian ales on tap. We worked our way through most of them and then feeling slightly squiffy headed to the local curry house at about 9:15. Mel had chicken tikka massala and I had spicy chicken jalfrezi with rice and a garlic naan each. We also had some poppadoms and a couple of kingfishers. All was lovely and mine had a right good kick to it! The bill was around £55. We staggered home for about 11pm.
