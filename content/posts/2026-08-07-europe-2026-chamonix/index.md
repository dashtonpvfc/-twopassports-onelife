Camp site: Öffentlicher Parkplatz Buet

Awoke from another poor night's sleep hanging off the cliff edge, all packed up and ready to leave Annecy around 11:30am. Headed to Lidl to pick up supplies - a couple of croissants, pain au chocolat, tomatoes, bread, ham and cream cheese to sustain us through the day's journey. 

![Leaving Annecy supplies](20260807_123654.jpg)

Headed to Natural campsite and motorhome location of Passy Plaine Joux aire which is about a 2 hour drive avoiding tolls. We also stopped at a beautiful boulangerie in Flumet climbing high into the French alps. 

![Boulangerie in Flumet](20260807_125525.jpg)
![Climbing into the French Alps](20260807_131346.jpg)
![Alps scenery](20260807_135049.jpg)
![Mountain drive](20260807_135107.jpg)

Weather was low 30's and full sun again. As we approached the road to the aire there were warning signs saying 1.9m wide max - the van is about 2.2m wide so we chanced it - we got about 200 metres up the mountain on a hairpin bend and a car came down towards us, then another, then a van, then another 3 vehicles. They couldn't all reverse up hill but there was also another 2 cars behind us. We got completely wedged. We had to shoehorn the van in a private driveway. 

![Hairpin bend jam](20260807_135736.jpg)
![Wedged in the driveway](20260807_135752.jpg)

It was too much so we gave up and headed downhill. Frantically searching for campsites and every single one was full. 

![Searching for campsites](20260807_145318.jpg)
![Mountain views](20260807_145454.jpg)
![Valley roads](20260807_145548.jpg)
![On the road](20260807_145656.jpg)
![Valley view](20260807_145658.jpg)

Finally dropped on a car park which turned out to be perfect for our simplistic needs - 5 euros for an overnight stop and nestled between mountains on all sides. 

![Car park stop](20260807_145702.jpg)
![Surrounding mountains](20260807_145704.jpg)
![Campsite views](20260807_151450.jpg)
![Mountain backdrop](20260807_151506.jpg)
![Parking spot](20260807_151512.jpg)

There was even a gorgeous quirky hotel bar next door, we headed there for a pint / Aperol or 3 and then headed back to the van around 6:30pm. 

![Hotel bar drinks](20260807_161351.jpg)
![Hotel bar view](20260807_163226.jpg)

We listened to the radio, sent the drone up for some footage, drank a couple of bottles of vin blanc and vin rouge and ate some lovely fromage and jambon and tomato toastie on thick sliced rustic crusty bread and real butter...très bien! 

![Drone shot](20260807_174026.jpg)
![Evening food and drinks](20260807_191211.jpg)

Mel sketched the local scenery with her new pencil set (she promises to do one a day here on in) and off to bed we popped with a slight nip in the air - the thermometer read 14 degrees - this called for the sleeping bag.
