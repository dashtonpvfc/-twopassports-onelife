## Day 1 - Wednesday 19 August

Change of plan from heading to Lake Garda next, the distances and heat are destroying us and would have left a humungous journey back so we picked a campsite on the German side of the Rhine separating Germany from Switzerland. Firstly filled up with fuel from Domaso, this was around £1.79 which is way cheaper than the ridiculous price of £2.40 as in Switzerland. 

![Fuel stop leaving Domaso](20260819_114828.jpg)

Again there was some absolutely stunning scenery along the way back through the Swiss Alps and Lake Lucerne. Unsurpassed with staggering beauty. The journey still ended up taking us over 6 hours in searing heat and some ridiculously long traffic jams - the first one into the 16km long Gotthard tunnel and the second on a Swiss motorway north of Zurich.

Eventually we arrived around 5pm at Rhein Camping Waldshut. What a glorious spot! Overlooking the Rhein river, we set up the awning and cracked a beer, Mel had a little snooze and I went for a wild swim in the Rhein - glorious! Perfect temperature and calm with a slight current downstream. The motorhomes here are of a different class - in particular a custom built Morelo Palace - going price around £600k!

We chose a lovely looking restaurant called "Mona Lisa" as it was now around 9pm and things were shutting down. I picked a Diavolo pizza - my third in 3 days, and Mel went for the lasagne with chips. Both were spot on. I also had a wheat beer and Mel a coke and two big bottles of San Pellegrino sparkling water. The bill was around £45, more expensive than Italy, but way cheaper than Switzerland.

We walked the road way home and arrived back around 10:30 - the van is still boiling hot - little midges everywhere as we are so close to the river so we had to close the blinds and roof - the electric fan has been an absolute godsend.

## Day 2 - Thursday 20 August

Gave Mel a lie in after a pretty shite night's sleep again and made myself a fantastic omelette and ate it overlooking the Rhein. 

![Breakfast omelette overlooking the Rhine](20260820_100633.jpg)

Left Mel at the site and went for a walk into Waldshut along the river. The weather is significantly cooler today - around 25 degrees and much needed. Stumbled upon a lovely cafe / bar - Stadtmürli Pils Pub, where I devoured the local beer made literally up the road - Rothaus Pils. I slowly sipped 3 of them whilst people watching and catching up on phone calls to the boys. Was there for a couple of hours and went to pay - cash only - I had no cash and no debit card, promised I would return to pay the 11 euro bar tab later. Wandered to the next bar for a small beer and grabbed a salted caramel ice cream to walk along the canal with. 

![Riverwalk path into Waldshut](20260820_125216.jpg)
![Stadtmürli Pils Pub in town](20260820_125224.jpg)
![Enjoying a local Rothaus Pils](20260820_132913.jpg)

Met sleepy Mel at the crazy golf place mid way back for a couple of beers and some heated debate about world affairs. 

![Crazy golf venue along river path](20260820_153727.jpg)
![Beers at the crazy golf bar](20260820_162519.jpg)

Walked to Lidl for some supplies as it was getting late and still hadn't ate when I spotted a Burger King! Not had a Burger King for about 20 years so thought I would try one. Had a double cheeseburger and bacon meal thing with fries smothered in cheese and jalapeños with extra chilli and a diet coke - it was actually really nice and filled a hole - cost was around £14. Wandered back, had an Erdinger Dunkel by the van before going to the big duvet.

## Day 3 - Friday 21 August

Weather today is dreadful. 19 degrees tops and hammering down. We have no food left, no milk and no coffee. Ran to the restaurant to grab a couple of cappuccinos to go - for 7 euros, they were absolutely tiny. Started reading my new book - Bullshit Jobs - a fascinating insight into the fakeness of the entire jobs industry. 

![Rainy morning over the Rhine](20260821_101438.jpg)
![Tiny takeaway cappuccinos](20260821_122013.jpg)

We dragged our sodden arses into the campsite restaurant for a German lunch. I had pork tsatsiki with fried potatoes and a lovely side salad. Mel had a feta and pepperoni Flammkuchen - a local flat bread with chips, I also had a lovely wheat beer and Mel a fruit tea. 

![Campsite restaurant interior](20260821_123843.jpg)
![German lunch spread with Flammkuchen and pork](20260821_130138.jpg)

We retired back to the van so Mel could have a sleep and I could read for a bit whilst it hammered down. It finally stopped raining around 5pm so we showered and wandered into town, first to pay my bar bill and then have a beer in said bar. 

![Rain clearing over campsite](20260821_164400.jpg)
![Returning to Stadtmürli Pils Pub to pay bill](20260821_190409.jpg)

We moved onto Calypso for a beer and an Aperol and then went for tea at Lamm German restaurant. What an absolute beauty of a building! Over 750 years old and full of character, solid old oak beams and great ales - it was right up our street. Mel had a Greek style Rosti with pork and olives and I had pork, mushrooms, and croquettes in a lovely red wine sauce. We pushed the boat out and had a dessert each - a Toblerone mousse & a creme brulee. We also had a local Riesling wine and a couple of Dunkels. Gorgeous all round - bill was about £45.

![Historic Lamm German restaurant building](20260821_195627.jpg)
![Interior oak beams at Lamm restaurant](20260821_195652.jpg)
![Traditional German dinner at Lamm](20260821_200705.jpg)

We walked back along the canal and were in bed by 21:30.
